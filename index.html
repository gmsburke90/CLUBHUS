01 The Idea

A clubhouse is not a bar. It is a standard — and it deserves a lager of its own.

CLUBHUS began with a simple conviction: the finest rooms in sport deserve more than the same lager poured everywhere else. So we brewed one that isn't. A clean, bright Danish pilsner made in small batches and placed only where membership means something.

You won't find us in supermarkets or on billboards. You'll find us behind the bar of your clubhouse — and increasingly, in the company you keep there.

02

The Lager · No. 01

Quietly exceptional. The way the best things are.

A bottom-fermented pilsner brewed with Danish floor-malted barley and noble hops. Bright gold, softly bitter, and built for the long afternoon.

  • NoseFresh bread, meadow, faint citrus
  • PalateCrisp, dry, clean bitterness

03

Membership

Belong to your club. Then belong to ours.

If you're a member of a partnered club, you can become a member of CLUBHUS — and your afternoons start working a little harder for you.

I

Join through your club

Members of partnered golf and sports clubs are invited into CLUBHUS — free, by association.

II

10% off every pour

Members enjoy a standing 10% on CLUBHUS at any partnered clubhouse. No fuss, no app required.

III

Earn points as you go

Every round and every pour earns points toward merch and partner rewards across travel, fashion and sport.

IV

Spend them well

Redeem on club jerseys or curated partner perks — hotels, lounges, car hire and more.
