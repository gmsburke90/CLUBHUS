Why we made it

Most beers want you to feel something about them. CLUBHUS doesn't. It's brewed for one specific moment, the first pour after the match, and built to do that one thing well.

No image. No statement. Just a clean, crisp lager that's already there when you walk off the course, the court, the river.

About

Same moment,
different game.

CLUBHUS started with a simple observation: the beer after the match rarely matches the moment. So we made one that does.

We're not chasing the hop arms race, and we're not borrowing sports culture as a vibe.

We're learning the actual rhythm of the clubhouse, the post-match quiet, the second beer with friends, one club at a time.
