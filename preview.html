For members

Once your club pours CLUBHUS, your members get more than a beer.

Sign up to CLUBHUS, and when your club is a partner, every pour earns you points worth 10% back, plus rewards from CLUBHUS and affiliate partners.

I

Sign up to start earning

Create a free CLUBHUS account. Once your club is a partner, every pour you order earns points, with nothing to scan at the bar.

II

Redeem for 10% back

Your points are worth the equivalent of 10% off CLUBHUS, so the discount lives in what you've earned, not a flat markdown at the till.

III

Or spend on affiliate rewards

The same points redeem in the CLUBHUS app, against CLUBHUS itself or deals from affiliate partners, the next thing we build once clubs are pouring and members are asking for it.

The 10% is unlocked by two things: your club is a partner, and you've signed up to CLUBHUS. The app and affiliate rewards are where it's headed, not all live today; we'll let members know the moment it's live at their club, see the form below.
