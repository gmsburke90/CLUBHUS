Export Lager takes the CLUBHUS standard and gives it more to say. A fuller malt backbone and a slightly higher gravity than the flagship lager, built the way export styles always were: with enough character to survive the trip and still taste right when it gets there.

It stays crisp. It stays sessionable. It just arrives with a little more weight in the glass.

The strain

Every CLUBHUS beer starts with a White Labs Copenhagen strain chosen for one job: staying clean under more malt. Export Lager needed a yeast that wouldn’t get lost under the extra body, so that’s where we started.

We’re not going to pretend to be brewers here. What matters to you: it’s a proper lager yeast, fermented cold and patient, so nothing gets in the way of the malt doing the talking.
