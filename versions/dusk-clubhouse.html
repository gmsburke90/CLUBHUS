01 — The Idea

A clubhouse isn't a bar. It's a standard — and it deserved a lager to match.

Every clubhouse pours the same beer as the pub down the road. We thought that was strange, so we brewed something else: a clean, quietly confident Danish lager, made in small batches, poured only behind bars where membership still means something.

You won't find CLUBHUS on a billboard or a supermarket shelf. You'll find it exactly where you'd hope to — in your hand, after the round, among people you chose to belong with.

02 — The Lager · No. 01

Quietly exceptional. The way clubhouses prefer it.

A bottom-fermented pilsner brewed with Danish floor-malted barley and noble hops. Pale gold, gently bitter, built to outlast the nineteenth hole.

  • StylePremium Pilsner Lager
  • NoseFresh bread, meadow, faint citrus
  • PalateCrisp, dry, clean bitterness
  • Serve3°C, tall glass, no rush

03 — Membership

Belong to your club. Then belong to ours.

Members of a partnered clubhouse are already halfway to CLUBHUS membership. The other half takes about a minute.

I

Walk in through your club

Members of partnered golf and sports clubs are invited in automatically. Free, by association.

II

Ten percent, always

A standing discount on every CLUBHUS pour at any partnered clubhouse. No card, no app, no fuss.

III

Points for every round

Every pour earns toward merch and a growing bench of partner rewards.

IV

Spend them properly

Redeem on club jerseys, or curated perks across travel, fashion and sport.
